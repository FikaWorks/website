Platform Build & Go-Live takes you from design to a production launch. We design
the platform with your team, build it as code, set up the pipelines and runbooks,
and stay beside you when it goes live. You finish with a platform your team
understands and can run.

Already have a platform? Start at go-live. We validate your architecture,
deployment readiness, operational processes and security posture before launch,
with readiness checks, pre-flight checklists, deployment rehearsals and
real-time support during cutover.

This service suits organisations launching a new platform, expanding an existing
deployment or adding multi-region and multi-cloud capabilities. It runs as a
fixed scope or as structured time and materials.
