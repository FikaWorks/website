The Readiness Review offers comprehensive validation, assessment, and
optimisation recommendations for establishing an optimal cloud-native platform
across private, public, or hybrid cloud infrastructures.

This service utilises a structured, interview-based methodology to thoroughly
review your current technological landscape.

The outcome is a detailed, expert assessment report, which includes specific,
actionable recommendations designed to facilitate your transition to a reliable
production environment.

A typical review covers one platform with up to two clusters and takes about
three working days. You get an architecture report, a security assessment and a
production readiness score. Choose the focus that fits your next step:
**platform**, **security**, **cost**, **sovereign** (before a move to a European
cloud) or **AI** (before running AI workloads in production).
