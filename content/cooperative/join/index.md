## How we work

The client signs one contract with FikaWorks. The cooperative handles contracts,
admin, leads and back office, and gives the members delivering the work matching
member terms.

Most of the client fee goes to the people delivering the work. A smaller share
funds cooperative operations and community activity. What remains goes to
members through patronage, linked to participation and contribution under
cooperative rules. An equal vote does not mean an equal payout.

Membership works both ways: members share knowledge, keep delivery standards
high and take part in cooperative life.
